HUMAN-

An AI generated and AI re-written article, on the prompt "Humans are the greatest threat to the future of technology".

Created as part of the "Critical Design" course led by Julijonas Urbonas at the Vilnius Art Academy, as part of my Sculpture (MA) studies.The brochure is a design prototype of what an article or study by AI about the dangers of humans to technology may look like. The work questions the current outlook on technological development, where tech comes first, even in all "human-centric" design approaches. The work questions whether we as humans should strive for this, by looking at the issue from the opposite side - how technology may perceive us as a threat.
