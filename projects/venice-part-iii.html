Venice, Part III

On a school trip to Venice, our main goal was to see the Biennale of 2013 - The Encyclopedic Palace. To me, the most interesting subjects were less the art itself, but more the textural and physical qualities of all the relationships in the city - the art, the people, the water, the light, and the materials.
