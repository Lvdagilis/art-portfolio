100th Anniversary of Lithuanian Statehood

As has become tradition (partly due to my interest in ethnosymbolism and just pure coincidence in timing), together with my parents I try to attend the flag raising ceremony in Vilnius, on the 16th of February - The Day of Restoration of Independence of Lithuania. I won't get into details, but really, it's less a restoration and more a creation of Lithuania as a modern nation. This year, 2018, was the 100th anniversary, the 100th birthday of our country. I took my trusty Bronica out to capture some of the moments of the day. Bronica ETRS, 150mm. Kodak TMax400. 120mm and 35mm (using 3D printed film adapters!). Self-developed in PQ Universal (1:29 for 8mins).
